Two ways to be early

Cheap, or
instant

One downloads the page. The other has already finished loading it.

  • Pick either, or run both and let them layer
  • Both prerenders a notch behind the prefetch, never ahead
  • So the expensive half is never spent on a passing pointer
Prefetch
A few kilobytes of HTML
The body is downloaded and kept. No subresources, no scripts, nothing on the page runs — and the navigation is still most of the way to instant.
Prerender
The page, already loaded
Fetched, rendered and executed in a hidden tab, so activating it is instant. It costs a whole page load that may never be used, which is exactly why the exclusions matter.

Eagerness

How keen is
too keen

Two hundred milliseconds of hover is a good proxy for having decided.

  • Chrome holds two at a time, discarding the oldest
  • So keener does not mean more — it means earlier, and wasted
  • Every speculative request carries Sec-Purpose, so measure it
conservativeon pointer or touch down2 at a time
moderate~200ms of hover, or pointerdown2 at a time
eager~10ms of hover2 at a time
immediateas soon as the rules are seen50 / 10

Default is moderate. The last row is the only one without a queue.

Who gets it

Signed in is
the risky one

A personalised page is the most expensive one you serve, and the likeliest to do something twice.

  • Guests on by default; signed-in visitors opt in
  • Admins are their own switch, so you can test on yourself
  • Or degrade: a prefetch runs nothing, and is nearly as quick

The checker

Ask it about
one link

Not “what are my rules”, but is it going to prerender the thing that signs people out.

  • Paste a URL in the CP, or ask on the command line
  • It names the exclusion that stopped it, not just the verdict
  • And admits what a URL alone cannot settle
$ php craft speculatr/rules/check /logout
  never speculated
  The logout URL (logoutPath)
$ php craft speculatr/rules/check /pricing
  prerender (moderate)
  unless the link matches .no-speculation,
  [rel~=nofollow], [download], [target=_blank]
