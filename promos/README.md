# Plugin Store promo images

Marketing images for the Speculatr listing on the Craft Plugin Store, rendered in the same theme as
the plugin's marketing page at
[justinholt.com/plugins/craft-speculatr](https://justinholt.com/plugins/craft-speculatr).

## Building

```bash
./build.sh          # all slides
./build.sh "2 5"    # just slides 2 and 5
```

Output lands in `out/` as `speculatr-promo-N.png`, 1920×1080 (rendered at 2× in headless Chrome, then
downsampled so the type stays crisp).

## Notes

Speculatr is **free with no editions**, so unlike the paid plugins in the family there is no price on
the cover badge and no figure anywhere in the deck that a pricing change could strand.

The accent is **`#4D7C0F`** (lime-700), picked by measuring ΔE against all 49 accent colours already
in the plugin registry — the lime family was the only genuinely open hue, and lime-700 is the
darkest of it that still clears 4.9:1 on white for the marketing page's body text. The brighter
limes look better on a dark slide and fail that, which is why the slides brighten it to `#84CC16`
and `#BEF264` for the gradients rather than using a lighter accent throughout.

The **watermark is an outline, not the filled mark** — the family trap. Speculatr's mark is already
mostly line-work, but filled at 1180px across a slide the three dashes still read as grey slugs
laid over the artwork. Strip the mass, keep the line.

Two layout rules, both inherited from the family deck and one of them re-learned here:

- **The copy column has to fit above the footer, and beside the panel.** The panel starts at
  `left: 900px` and the copy column is 780px inside a 130px gutter. Two-line headings, three-or-four
  line ledes, and one line per bullet (about 60 characters at 25px).
- **`.points li` is a flex container.** An inline `<span class="mono">` or `<em>` left loose in the
  text becomes a *sibling flex item* and picks up the 16px gap on both sides. Every point keeps its
  text in one `<span class="t">`.

And one found while rendering slide 6:

- **A `white-space: nowrap` flex item will not shrink, and shoves its siblings off the panel.**
  A flex item's default `min-width: auto` floors it at its content width, so a long `.what` pushed
  the `.state` label clean past the panel's right edge and off the slide — nothing clipped, it
  simply kept going. `min-width: 0` lets it give up space; dropping `nowrap` lets it wrap rather
  than overflow. The copy still has to be short enough not to wrap, or that row ends up taller than
  the others.

`fonts.css` is generated by `build.sh`, which base64-inlines the two woff2 files in `assets/` —
Chrome's `file://` origin rules block them otherwise. It is gitignored; don't edit it.
